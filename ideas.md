# ESUT Marketplace Design Direction

**ESUT campus-commerce** should feel energetic, trusted, student-run, and locally specific rather than like a generic ecommerce template.

- Green communicates trust, community, verification, and campus belonging.
- Red communicates commerce, decisive actions, and current deals.
- Gold is reserved for premium emphasis and limited promotional accents.
- Product presentation must use real seller-uploaded images where available. When an image is unavailable, the UI should use an intentional category-led fallback rather than imply a product photograph exists.
- Discovery shelves should maintain distinct editorial roles: urgency for deals, social proof for trending, student relevance for campus picks, and safety for verified sellers.
